Bench check

Connect your transmitter

This sim flies on rate mode with Betaflight-style curves, so the muscle memory you build here transfers to a real quad. It reads your actual radio through the browser's gamepad interface — no drivers, no install.

01
Plug the radio into this computer with a USB-C or micro-USB data cable. Charge-only cables won't enumerate.
02
On the radio's screen choose USB Joystick (HID). EdgeTX and OpenTX both prompt on connect — RadioMaster, Jumper, TX16S, FrSky Taranis, Horus.
03
Move any stick. Browsers hide gamepads until they see input, so nothing appears until you nudge a gimbal. Until then the keyboard flies, and the radio takes over the moment it shows up.
04
Run the stick calibration below, then arm with the Enter key or your AUX switch.
